# CARE Lab

**Climate Action and Resilience for Equity**

CARE Lab is a research group led by Assistant Professor Seona Park at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). We study climate change and energy transitions from a social science perspective, with a focus on the people, communities, and institutions shaping a low-carbon future.

Our name reflects the questions that guide our work. **Climate Action** concerns reducing carbon emissions. **Resilience** emphasizes adaptation and recovery in a warming world. **Equity** places the experiences and needs of marginalized communities at the center of climate and energy policy.

**Website:** [seonaparklab.github.io](https://seonaparklab.github.io/)

## Research Focus

- **Energy justice and just transition:** How can climate and energy policies support fair and inclusive transitions?
- **Renewable energy and local communities:** Social impacts, public acceptance, and stakeholder participation in renewable energy development, including offshore wind power.
- **Coal phase-out and carbon lock-in:** The implications of moving away from coal for workers, local communities, and policy institutions.
- **Place attachment and collaborative planning:** How relationships with places and community participation inform environmental decisions.
- **Climate communication and resilience:** How people understand climate change and engage with mitigation and adaptation.
