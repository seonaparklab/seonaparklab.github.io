On 1 September 2026, Jing YU led an online “Climate Change 101” sharing session for participants from diverse regions, including Tibetan areas. The session connected climate science and governance with local observations of glacier retreat, grassland degradation, and changing livelihoods on the Tibetan Plateau. Participants discussed mitigation, adaptation, and community action, exploring how scientific knowledge and local experience can inform responses to climate change.
