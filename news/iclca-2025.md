<p><time datetime="2025-10-22">22 October 2025</time></p>

Professor Seona PARK and PhD student Zhuotao LU attended the 11th International Conference on Low Carbon Asia (ICLCA 2025) in Kuching, Malaysia, on 22 October 2025.

Professor Seona PARK chaired the parallel session Climate Justice & Inclusive Transition.

Zhuotao LU presented her research titled “Provincial Climate Policy Uncertainty and Differentiated ESG Greenwashing: Evidence from Chinese A-share Companies”.
